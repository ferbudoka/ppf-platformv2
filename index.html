FAQ

Common Questions

The PPF Navigator is a tool developed by the Global Infrastructure Facility (GIF) to help governments, project sponsors, and advisors find the right Project Preparation Facility (PPF) for their infrastructure projects in emerging markets. It consolidates data from dozens of facilities into a single searchable, filterable platform.
The platform is designed for government officials, project developers, private sector sponsors, development finance institutions, and infrastructure advisors who need to identify and access project preparation support across emerging markets.
Use the pill filters on the left panel to narrow results by Preparation Stage, Region, Support Type, Sector, and Financing Instrument. Filters apply instantly — the map, cards, and table all update together. Click "Reset All Filters" to start over.
Each marker represents the headquarters of a Project Preparation Facility. When multiple facilities share a location, a numbered cluster circle appears. Zoom in or click the cluster to expand and see individual markers. Hover over any marker to see a summary; click to open the full detail panel.
The database covers five support types: Grants, Technical Assistance, Equity, Loans, and Advisory services. Each facility may offer one or more types. Use the Support Type filter to match your specific needs.
Click on any facility card or map marker to open the full detail panel, then click the "Contact" button to reach the facility directly. You can also export the full list as a CSV from the Facilities page for offline reference.
Yes. Visit the All Facilities page and click "Export CSV" to download the full dataset. The export includes all columns: facility name, organisation, region, country, sector, support type, instrument, stage, and funding amount.
Open the Dashboard and click "Submit Fund" in the top toolbar. Fill in the structured form with the facility's details. All submissions are reviewed by the GIF team within 5–7 business days before being added to the database.

A tool of the Global Infrastructure Facility — a G20 initiative supporting sustainable infrastructure in emerging markets through preparation finance and advisory services.
